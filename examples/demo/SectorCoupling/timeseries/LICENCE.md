Contents of files in this folder were derived from data available at external sources, namely

1. [BMWK](https://www.bmwk.de)
2. [Destatis](https://www.destatis.de)
3. [EEX](https://www.eex.com)
4. [SMARD](https://www.smard.de)  

and additional own calculations.
